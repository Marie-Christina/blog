## History & Culture
### Bridgestone Museum of Art
- Bridgestone (now Ishibashi Foundation) was founded by **Shojiro Ishibashi** in Kurume in 1931
  - "Ishibashi" literally means "stone bridge" → Bridgestone (a translation/inversion of his name)
- Ishibashi was a massive patron of Western and Japanese art → donated collection to establish the museum
- The Ishibashi Foundation Museum in Kurume holds Impressionist and modern Western works alongside Japanese paintings — remarkable for a smaller Japanese city
